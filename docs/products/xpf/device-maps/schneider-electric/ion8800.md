# Schneider Electric ION8800 Modbus Register Map

The Schneider Electric ION8800 is a power meter used for power monitoring, energy metering, and facility automation. This page provides a sample Modbus register map with addresses, data types, and signal categories to help engineers commission, troubleshoot, and monitor the device. For Schneider Electric ION8800 deployments, teams often use this map to surface power, power factor, and energy data in data centers, switchgear lineups, and advanced power monitoring projects.
